RESEARCH REQUEST: WHEN THE TREATMENT IS ALSO THE ONLY SOURCE OF BETWEEN-UNIT
VARIANCE, AND THE OUTCOME COLLAPSES TO A SINGLE USABLE DIMENSION

Date: 2026-08-19


0. HOW TO READ THIS REQUEST

We ran a simulation experiment, it produced a null, and we then audited the
machine rather than the result. The audit found three structural facts we did
not design for. We want to know what the literature calls them, whether the
design is salvageable, and what is already known about each.

What we need:
(a) whether an untreated control arm that cannot vary internally is still a
    valid control, and what that situation is called,
(b) mechanisms that create within-population heterogeneity WITHOUT being the
    treatment itself and without assigning traits,
(c) known pathologies of an outcome that is (i) selected by an argmax over
    axes and (ii) written only above a threshold,
(d) whether the shrinking of successive fine-tuning updates, when each
    generation continues from its parent's weights, is a named phenomenon.

What we do NOT need:
- Our project history, or a design proposal for our specific system.
- Any advice that depends on how large our effects are. We have not looked at
  the between-arm contrast for this purpose and will not: choosing a design by
  anticipating its effect is post-hoc.
- Code.

1. THE SYSTEM

A simulation of inheritance across generations, structured as a controlled
experiment with three arms. Each arm has its own population of N = 8 agents
and its own shared resource pool; arms never interact.

An agent lives a sequence of discrete events. At each event it announces a
withdrawal from the pool, the pool serves the announced withdrawals ONE AGENT
AT A TIME in a rotating order, and whatever it actually received feeds it.
At the end of a generation, agents are ranked by a fitness quantity, winners
of a size-2 tournament produce heirs, population size stays fixed, and the
number of heirs per parent is the selection weight w.

Three arms:
- "lived": the agent's own experience is distilled into a preference dataset
  and fine-tuned into a small per-agent adapter, which its heir inherits;
- "shuffle": the SAME data with the preference pairing destroyed, fine-tuned
  the same way, inherited the same way;
- "null": no fine-tuning at all. Heirs still inherit the symbolic memory
  store.

Everything is deterministic: the same seed reproduces bit-for-bit. Different
seeds are normal (an earlier study ran 40).


2. WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND

FINDING 1 - the treatment is the only source of variance.

Agents are born identical within an arm. The intended differentiator was the
sequential, rotating service of the pool: whoever is served last gets what the
others left. Measured, that only bites when the pool is actually short. If the
stock covers every announced withdrawal, order changes nothing and identical
agents stay identical.

The pool only runs short when the agents demand differently, and they only
demand differently when their weights differ - that is, when the fine-tuned
adapter differs. So:

    adapter -> different behaviour -> different demand -> scarcity
            -> sequential service bites -> heterogeneity

In one probe, the "null" arm's eight agents were identical in EVERY recorded
quantity across all three generations (harvest spread 0, energy spread 0,
lifespan all equal, fitness spread 0). Its pool never fell below 59 percent of
capacity and it never had a scarcity event. The two fine-tuned arms
differentiated normally in the same seed.

So the untreated control arm can be a frozen clone population: its fitness has
zero variance, its tournament is a coin flip, and any outcome measured on it
has zero within-group variance by construction.

FINDING 2 - the outcome has one usable dimension out of four.

The outcome is a "drift" vector with four named domains. Two things reduce it:

(a) the domain is chosen by an argmax over which internal axis moved most in
    that event. One axis (energy) moves at every single event because of
    metabolism, so it almost always wins the argmax. Measured over 216 lives,
    only two of the four domains were ever written, and two were written zero
    times.
(b) a domain is written only when the event's magnitude reaches a fixed
    threshold. Measured, the individual channel crossed that threshold in 11
    percent of lives; observed peaks sit just below it.

In addition, one of the two domains that IS written is the domain used by a
group-wide scarcity shock, which writes the same value into every agent of the
arm at once. So the individually-informative part of the outcome is
effectively one domain, present in 5 percent of readings.

FINDING 3 - the parametric channel shrinks each generation.

Each heir starts from its parent's adapter weights and then trains on its own
life. The size of the weight change per generation fell monotonically in six
of six lineages, by factors of 1.8x to 4.8x over three generations.


3. CONSTRAINTS

C1. No trait, disposition or strategy may be assigned to an agent, and the
    agents' decision rule may not be edited. The claim under test is that
    structure emerges from living rather than from being told.
C2. All measurements are deterministic computations; no model-as-judge.
C3. Any constant we change must be derivable from other constants by a stated
    inequality, fixed before the run, and never chosen by looking at the
    outcome.
C4. Small numbers: 8 agents per arm per generation, 2 selection episodes per
    seed, and a run costs hours of GPU time.


4. THE QUESTIONS

Q1. A CONTROL THAT CANNOT VARY.
Our untreated arm has zero within-group variance in every measured quantity,
because the treatment is also what generates heterogeneity. Is such an arm
still a valid control, and is this situation named in the methodological
literature (for example as a treatment that affects the variance rather than
the mean, or as a control condition that is degenerate by construction)? What
is the accepted way to report a comparison in which one arm's outcome has zero
variance by construction rather than by measurement?

Q2. TREATMENT-INDEPENDENT HETEROGENEITY.
What mechanisms are used in agent-based or evolutionary simulation to generate
within-population heterogeneity among initially identical agents WITHOUT
assigning traits and WITHOUT the differentiator being the experimental
treatment itself? We are aware of positional and order effects in sequential
resource access; our finding is that these are conditional on scarcity. We are
looking for mechanisms whose differentiating power does not vanish when
resources are abundant, with sources.

Q3. AN OUTCOME THAT IS BOTH ARGMAX-SELECTED AND THRESHOLDED.
Our outcome vector records only the axis that moved most, and only when the
movement exceeds a threshold. Are the pathologies of such a construction
described anywhere (loss of dimensionality, dominance of a fast-moving axis,
information destroyed below the threshold)? What alternatives are used when a
multi-dimensional internal state must be summarised into a comparable outcome,
and what do they assume?

Q4. SHRINKING SUCCESSIVE UPDATES IN INHERITED PARAMETERS.
When each generation resumes fine-tuning from its parent's parameters, the
per-generation weight change shrinks monotonically. Is this a named phenomenon
(for example loss of plasticity, saturation, or a consequence of repeated
low-rank updates), what causes it, and what is known about whether the
transmitted signal itself decays with it?

Q5. MINIMAL EVIDENCE FOR CUMULATIVE INHERITANCE.
In simulation studies claiming that an acquired change accumulates across
generations rather than merely appearing once, what is the accepted minimum
evidence - how many generations, what has to be shown about the control, and
what claims does that evidence license? We want the reporting standard, not a
design for our system.
